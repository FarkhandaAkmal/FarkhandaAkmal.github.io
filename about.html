About me

GIS, remote sensing and land information work in Punjab, Pakistan.

GIS and remote sensing

Connecting spatial information with place

I am a GIS and remote sensing professional based in Lahore, working on land records and cadastral mapping in Punjab since 2012. I currently manage GIS work on the Punjab Urban Land Systems Enhancement (PULSE) project.

Before that I spent more than ten years at The Urban Unit as a Research Associate in GIS and remote sensing, contributing to projects in land record management, property tax mapping, spatial planning and crop mapping. I hold an MPhil in Geomatics from the University of the Punjab, and my research on urban growth, site selection, solar potential and flood damage assessment has been published in international journals.
